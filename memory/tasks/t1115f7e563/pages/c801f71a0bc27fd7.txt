## Mati Staniszewski & Piotr Dąbkowski – ElevenLabs
- founded 2022
- HQ London
- Sector AI
- Region Greater London
ElevenLabs is an AI research and product company transforming how we interact with technology. It was co-founded in 2022 in London by childhood friends from Poland Mati Staniszewski (CEO) and Piotr Dąbkowski (CTO). ElevenLabs is reported to be in early talks with investors about a secondary share sale that could value the company at around $22bn.
