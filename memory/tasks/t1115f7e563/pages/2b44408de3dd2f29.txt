ElevenLabs launched its public beta in January 2023 with English text-to-speech and voice cloning. Early releases introduced multilingual speech models, faster voice generation, AI dubbing, and tools for long-form audio production.
The company later introduced sound effects, conversational voice agents, Scribe transcription models, and AI music generation. This timeline documents major model and product releases from the first public beta onward, including research previews, API launches, general availability, and significant platform changes.
Last updated: November 12, 2026.

## Frequently asked questions

## When did ElevenLabs first launch?
ElevenLabs launched its public beta on January 23, 2023, introducing text-to-speech and voice-cloning tools. The company was founded in 2022.

## When was Eleven v3 released?
Eleven v3 debuted as an alpha research preview on June 3, 2025. Public API access followed on August 20, 2025, and the model became generally available on February 2, 2026.

## How do ElevenLabs speech model versions differ from Voice Design versions?
Speech models generate spoken audio from text. Voice Design generates the voice identity used for that speech. Eleven v3 and Voice Design v3 are different model families.

## Is ElevenAgents a single speech-to-speech model?
ElevenAgents is a platform for voice and chat agents. It uses speech recognition, language models, speech synthesis, and conversation controls. The platform launched as Conversational AI before becoming ElevenLabs Agents and later ElevenAgents.

## How does ElevenMusic differ from the Eleven Music models?
Eleven Music refers to the music-generation model family introduced in August 2025. ElevenMusic is the listening and creation platform introduced in April 2026. Music v2 and Music v2.5 are later generations of the music model.
