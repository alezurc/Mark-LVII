## What Is ElevenLabs?
ElevenLabs is a London-based company that develops voice and audio generation technology. It builds AI models that generate natural-sounding speech, recognize spoken language, reproduce voices, translate audio and create other forms of audio content.
The company was founded in 2022 by Piotr Dąbkowski, a former Google machine learning engineer, and Mateusz Staniszewski, an ex-Palantir strategist. The Polish co-founders initially set out to improve the quality of film dubbing, which led them to develop the backbone of their audio AI models. The company is best known for developing text-to-speech technology that generates natural-sounding speech, complete with pauses and emotional expression. It has since broadened its offerings to include voice cloning, conversational AI agents, music, sound effects and speech transcription. Its models can now speak, listen and translate in more than 90 languages.
ElevenLabs’ technology was initially popular with creators, but it has become increasingly popular with enterprise customers, which now make up about 55 percent of its roughly $600 million in annual recurring revenue . ElevenLabs’ conversational agents handle more than 15 million conversations per week, disbursing refunds, renewing insurance policies and upgrading phone plans on behalf of the world’s largest tech, insurance and telecom companies. The company announced in September 2026 that it is valued at $22 billion, and it has been reported the company aims to go public in 2028.

## ElevenAgents
ElevenAgents is a platform for building, deploying and managing conversational AI agents that can provide customer support and other tasks. An AI agent could, for example, answer customer questions, schedule appointments or help process routine service requests, escalating more complex tasks to a human employee.
Businesses can configure agents with instructions, connect them to external tools and software, and establish guardrails to influence how they respond. The company says its agents can handle interruptions, manage conversational turn-taking and ground responses in a knowledge base specific to that company.
ElevenLabs also offers Reception AI, a purpose-built, ready-to-deploy agent that can handle many of the duties of a phone receptionist, such as fielding incoming calls and other routine administrative tasks.

## ElevenAPI
ElevenAPI allows software developers to integrate text-to-speech, transcription and other generative audio capabilities into their own applications via API . This gives developers the technical control, flexibility and customization they need to create a specific software product.
For example, a developer building a language learning app could integrate ElevenLabs’ API to let users listen to vocabulary, lessons and example conversations in natural-sounding voices. The app could also use speech-to-text to transcribe the language learner’s spoken responses. Both features would work directly within the app’s existing interface, with ElevenLabs providing the underlying audio technology through its API.

## Concerns About ElevenLabs’ Voice AI
Despites its technological innovation and business benefits, ElevenLabs' voice AI  technology has drawn scrutiny over its potential for fraud and misinformation, its data privacy practices and its impact on working voice actors.

## Fraud and Misinformation
Voice cloning can create an audio recording of someone saying something they never actually said, which could be used to facilitate fraud or spread misinformation.
In 2024, ElevenLabs came under fire when a political consultant cloned then-President Joe Biden’s voice for a robocall campaign that discouraged New Hampshire voters from voting in the presidential primary . The Federal Communications Commission imposed a $6 million fine on the political consultant responsible. ElevenLabs, meanwhile, has restricted clones of certain public figures’ voices and required consent confirmation for voice cloning. It’s also developed an AI Speech Classifier for checking whether audio may have been generated using its technology.
Elected officials have publicly questioned what ElevenLabs and other AI voice companies are doing to prevent scammers from cloning someone’s voice, like a friend or family member, to obtain money or gain access to sensitive information.

## Privacy and Data Handling
By default, ElevenLabs retains voice recordings and other personal data to train its AI models. The company says it does not use data from enterprise customers for AI training, and on some products, it offers a “Zero Retention Mode” that deletes data once the request is completed. The company also gives non-enterprise users the option to opt out of sharing their data for AI training.
Similar to other AI companies, ElevenLabs uses publicly available or third-party information to train its models, which has raised concerns about unauthorized usage. In May 2026, a group of Chicago journalists, podcasters and voice actors sued ElevenLabs and eight other big tech companies for allegedly using their voices to train AI models. Filing their lawsuit in Illinois, which has the strongest biometric privacy laws in the country, the plaintiffs claimed ElevenLabs and other tech companies stole their “voiceprint,” which they say is a mathematical representation of their unique voice.

## Undermining Artists
When actor Michael Caine agreed to have his voice cloned for an audiobook retelling of Homer’s “The Odyssey,” some in the media industry began to wonder if AI audiobooks would become a trend that would undermine the value of working voice actors.
But AI audiobooks haven't reduced the demand for voice actors yet, as many still find actors to be more emotive than even ElevenLabs’ most advanced models. Only 16 percent of audiobook listeners have reported listening to an AI-voiced audiobook, according to an Audio Publishers Association survey , and AI audiobooks made up less than half of a percent of the audiobook industry’s revenue in 2025.

## Is ElevenLabs Safe to Use?
ElevenLabs can be used safely for many legitimate creative and business purposes, but safety depends on how the technology is used and what information users provide.
Perhaps the biggest area of concern is data privacy. ElevenLabs says that the company may retain and train its AI on user data unless users have opted out or are using an enterprise-level subscription. Users should be mindful of these policies, particularly if they are submitting confidential business information or personal details. Businesses handling regulated information should verify that their chosen product and contractual arrangements meet their security and compliance requirements.

## Notable ElevenLabs Developments
Since its inception, ElevenLabs has moved quickly — establishing itself as one of the best known synthetic speech companies, expanding from voice cloning into broader audio generation and scaling rapidly into an enterprise business. Below is a timeline of the company’s most significant developments.

## ElevenLabs Enables SoundCloud Publishing (November 2026)
ElevenLabs rolled out an integration with SoundCloud that lets users publish generated music and audio directly to the streaming platform. The feature automatically embeds provenance and AI-origin metadata into uploaded tracks and gives creators a direct distribution pipeline to listeners without having to export and manually re-upload their audio files.

## ElevenLabs Doubles Valuation to $22 Billion (September 2026)
ElevenLabs reached a $22 billion valuation after closing a $300 million employee secondary tender offer led by Wellington Management and T. Rowe Price. The deal doubled the company’s valuation, driven by rapid enterprise adoption of its conversational voice agents. The company noted the transaction was designed to provide liquidity to staff as it prepares for a potential public offering.

## ElevenLabs Reaches $11 Billion Valuation (February 2026)
ElevenLabs closed a $500 million Series D funding round that pushed its valuation to $11 billion. The round was led by Sequoia Capital and included participation from existing investors Andreessen Horowitz and ICONIQ. The funding followed a surge in enterprise adoption for automated customer service agents, video game voice acting and licensed voice clones in commercial media.

## Scribe Model Release (February 2025)
ElevenLabs expanded into speech recognition with the release of Scribe , its first foundational speech-to-text model. Built for transcription and low-latency audio processing, the model allowed developers to handle both transcription and voice synthesis through a single provider. The Scribe model made it significantly easier to build fully automated, real-time voice agents on ElevenLabs' platform.

## ElevenLabs Raises $80 Million (January 2025)
ElevenLabs secured $80 million in a Series B round co-led by Andreessen Horowitz, Nat Friedman, Daniel Gross and ICONIQ Growth. The raise coincided with the rollout of an expanded product ecosystem, including an automated dubbing studio, a marketplace for licensed synthetic voices, and mobile reader applications.

## Multilingual v2 Release (August 2023)
ElevenLabs exited its initial beta period by rolling out Multilingual v2 , expanding its text-to-speech engine to nearly 30 languages. The update allowed users to generate speech in different languages while keeping the original speaker's accent, pitch and delivery style consistent across all of them.

## ElevenLabs Founded (2022)
Childhood friends Piotr Dąbkowski, a former Google machine learning engineer, and Mati Staniszewski, an ex-Palantir strategist, co-founded ElevenLabs . Inspired by poorly dubbed Hollywood movies they watched growing up in Poland, the duo set out to build human-quality synthetic voice models capable of matching realistic emotion, pacing and nuanced delivery.

## Frequently Asked Questions

## What is ElevenLabs?
ElevenLabs is an artificial intelligence company that develops tools for generating, editing and processing audio. Its products help creators produce voiceovers, businesses automate customer service conversations and developers integrate AI-powered voice capabilities into their applications.

## Is ElevenLabs an AI voice generator?
Yes. ElevenLabs is best known for its AI voice-generation technology, which converts written text into natural-sounding speech. It also offers voice cloning, speech-to-text transcription, AI dubbing, music generation, sound effects and conversational AI tools.

## Can ElevenLabs clone voices?
Yes. ElevenLabs offers AI voice cloning that can reproduce a person's vocal characteristics and generate new speech in that voice. Instant Voice Cloning is available on paid Starter plans and above, while Professional Voice Cloning requires a higher-tier subscription. Users must prove they have appropriate authorization to clone a voice.

## Is ElevenLabs free to use?
ElevenLabs offers a free plan with 10,000 monthly credits and access to features such as text-to-speech, speech-to-text, voice design and sound effects. However, the free plan has usage limits and does not include a commercial license.

## Can ElevenLabs be used commercially?
Yes, but generally only content generated under an eligible paid subscription can be used commercially. ElevenLabs' free plan permits noncommercial use with attribution, while paid plans include commercial licensing, subject to applicable terms, intellectual property rights and restrictions. Certain beta features may be excluded.
